# Demo 3 — End-to-End Story (the close)

**Audience:** prospective clients who've already seen Demos 1 and 2 separately, or a shorter combined pitch when time is tight
**Length:** ~5 minutes
**Setup:** two screens/windows if possible — phone (or phone-emulator) for WhatsApp, laptop for the dashboard, side by side. This is the demo that sells the *system*, not just the features.

---

## The pitch in one line

> "Watch — I'm going to do one thing on my phone, and you're going to watch it appear on the office screen with nothing in between."

## The sequence

1. **On the phone**, send: *"clocking in at elmwood"*
   → **On the dashboard**, already sitting on `/field-time`: refresh, point at the new entry appearing for Tyler/Mia at 88 Elmwood Crescent.
   - **Say:** "No sync button, no 'refresh from the field app.' Same database, immediately."

2. **On the phone**, send a photo of a receipt with a note: *"fuel receipt, $85"*
   → **On the dashboard**, switch to **Notifications** — the pending spend record shows up in the Inbox for approval.
   - **Say:** "It doesn't just log — it routes to the person who's supposed to approve it. That's the whole 'confirm-before-execute' idea in one screenshot."

3. **On the dashboard**, click into **Equipment**, point at the idle-vehicle alert on SDBY-204.
   → **On the phone**, send: *"whats going on with truck 204"*
   → Bot answers from the same alert data.
   - **Say:** "The office raised it automatically from telemetry. The crew can ask about it in plain English and get the same answer an admin sees on screen."

4. **Close on the dashboard's Home page**, scrolled to **Recent activity**.
   - **Say:** "Every single thing I just did — from a $6 phone with a text app — is one line in this feed, permanently. If a customer ever disputes a job, if payroll ever gets questioned, if an insurance adjuster ever asks 'where was that truck at 2pm' — it's here, not in someone's memory."

## The close line

> "This isn't a dashboard with a chatbot bolted on. It's one system — the phone and the office are just two windows into the same truth."

---

### If you only have 3 minutes
Cut to just step 1 and step 4. Check-in → dashboard update → recent-activity feed is the whole story in miniature.
